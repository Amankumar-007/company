9. AI should not invent SEO conclusions

This is extremely important.

Your AI should never say:

Google penalized your website.

unless your system has actual evidence.

Instead:

Organic clicks decreased 21% compared with the previous period. The largest decline occurred on five pages targeting commercial keywords. Further investigation is recommended.

The AI should interpret your collected data, not manufacture explanations.

✓ Weekly comparison

Get the first version working with:

GSC + GA4 + Puppeteer + PageSpeed + AI + PDF + email.

Then add the expensive features after people actually use it.
